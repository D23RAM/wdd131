Gods & Goddesses

Hindu tradition describes a vast family of deities, each representing different qualities of the same underlying divine reality. This page starts with the Trimurti, the three central roles of creation, preservation, and destruction, then explores three of Vishnu's avatars, and finishes with a few other widely beloved deities.
