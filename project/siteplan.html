Site Planning Document

Site Name

DharmaVerse

DharmaVerse combines the Sanskrit word Dharma, meaning righteousness and duty, with Verse, representing sacred scriptures and knowledge. The name reflects a welcoming place where visitors can learn about Hindu beliefs, traditions, festivals, and philosophy.

Optional Domain: dharmaverse.org

Site Purpose

DharmaVerse is an educational website designed to help people learn about Hinduism. Visitors can explore the religion's beliefs, gods and goddesses, scriptures, festivals, and traditions through easy-to-read content and interactive features.

The website will include:

  • Introduction to Hinduism
  • Major Hindu gods and goddesses
  • Important scriptures
  • Hindu festivals
  • Interactive quiz
  • Contact form

Target Audience

  • Students
  • Anyone interested in Hinduism
  • Visitors planning to visit Hindu temples
  • People who want to learn about Indian culture

Visitor Scenarios

  1. What are the basic beliefs of Hinduism?
  2. Who are the major Hindu gods and goddesses?
  3. Which Hindu festivals are celebrated throughout the year?
  4. Where can I learn more about the Bhagavad Gita?

Website Pages

  • Home – Introduction to Hinduism
  • Gods & Goddesses – Information about major deities
  • Festivals & Scriptures – Festivals and sacred texts
  • Quiz & Contact – Interactive quiz and contact form
